Privacy Policy

BLOCK Arcade · Effective October 2, 2026

BLOCK Arcade is an offline puzzle game developed by Dmytro Husiev.

Data in the app

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking services. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Local storage

Personal best scores are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer. Removing the app deletes its local data.

Apple services

If you install the app through the App Store or TestFlight, Apple handles installation, distribution and any diagnostics you choose to share under Apple's privacy policy. The app does not include a separate diagnostics SDK.

Support

If you email us, we use the information you provide only to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive personal information. Contact: dmytro.gusev@gmail.com.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
